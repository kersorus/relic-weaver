# Asset prompts — Relic Weaver 1.2

Все семь production-ассетов созданы встроенным `imagegen`, затем приведены к размерам проекта с сохранением альфа-канала. Для предметов и персонажей использовался изолированный объект без тени; окружение создавалось сразу в портретной композиции.

## Общая арт-дирекция

`Handcrafted high-detail 32-bit pixel art for a dark gothic fantasy RPG; deliberate square pixel clusters, crisp stepped edges, near-black navy, muted cyan, deep violet and restrained antique-gold highlights; no text, logos or watermark.`

## Предметы

- `Игла свободы`: silver ritual sewing needle, pale-cyan magical thread loop, small gold eye and violet rune glints; diagonal centered inventory silhouette.
- `Часы отлива`: compact antique-brass hourglass, violet sand, pale-blue glass and a cyan tide rune; centered upright inventory silhouette.
- `Якорная печать`: dark-steel anchor fused into a tarnished-brass medallion, cyan tide rune and a short snapped chain; centered protective relic.

## Противники

- `Соляной паломник`: drowned medieval traveler in dark robes and salt-crusted plate, hooded black face with cyan eyes and one pilgrim staff.
- `Обсидиановая манта`: compact floating manta made of faceted black volcanic glass, cyan cracks and violet eyes; the tail was corrected in a targeted image edit.
- `Несвязанный хор`: tall midnight-robed boss with three cracked ivory masks, severed cyan/violet ribbons and one broken golden halo.

## Окружение

- `Берег оборванных дорог`: 9:16 moonlit coast, broad empty cobblestone foreground, road sinking into a black sea, broken arches, severed magical threads and a distant ring lighthouse.
